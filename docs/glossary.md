# Glossary

**Project:** Glyph Garden  
**Language:** Grammar of the Seasons  
**Document Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Draft

---

## Purpose

This glossary defines the shared vocabulary of the Grammar of the Seasons.

Terms appearing here have specific meanings within Glyph Garden and should be used consistently throughout the project's documentation.

Where possible, definitions are independent of any individual glyph, season, or implementation.

---

# A

## Assembly

A composition formed by combining multiple Modules into a larger geometric structure.

Assemblies preserve the identity of their constituent Modules while creating more complex forms.

---

# C

## Center

The primary point from which a glyph is constructed.

The Center establishes orientation, balance, and the origin of growth.

Every glyph possesses one principal Center.

---

## Constraint

A deliberate limitation that defines the space within which exploration occurs.

Constraints simplify decision-making and encourage coherent variation.

---

## Construction Plate

A focused exploration that introduces exactly one significant geometric concept.

Construction Plates document discovery rather than finished design.

---

## Construction Universe

The geometric environment in which a glyph exists.

The Construction Universe establishes orientation, scale, symmetry, and available construction space.

---

# D

## Design Decision

A documented choice that influences the evolution of the language.

Design Decisions record *why* an approach was adopted, providing historical context for future contributors.

---

# E

## Exploratory Parameter

A parameter under active investigation.

Exploratory Parameters are experimental and may eventually become Seasonal or Foundational Parameters.

---

# F

## Foundation

The collection of stable geometric concepts shared by every glyph in the language.

Foundational concepts evolve slowly and define the architectural identity of the Grammar.

---

## Foundational Parameter

A stable parameter that applies across the entire language rather than to individual glyphs.

Examples include the coordinate system, hierarchy, and growth model.

---

## Grammar

The complete system of geometric principles, structures, constraints, and relationships that governs how glyphs may be constructed.

The Grammar defines possibilities rather than finished forms.

---

## Growth

The process by which new geometry emerges from existing geometry.

Growth is constructive rather than additive.

New forms extend or transform previous structures instead of appearing independently.

---

## Growth Station

A designated location from which additional geometry may emerge.

A Growth Station represents possibility, not obligation.

---

# G

## Glyph

A complete visual expression created using the Grammar of the Seasons.

A glyph represents the culmination of one or more Assemblies organized into a coherent whole.

---

# H

## Hierarchy

The visual organization of geometric elements according to structural importance.

Hierarchy may be expressed through scale, branching depth, spacing, repetition, or other geometric relationships.

---

# M

## Module

A reusable geometric construction representing one coherent structural idea.

Modules are the primary building blocks from which Assemblies are formed.

---

## Module Sheet

A structured exploration of a single Module across multiple variations.

Module Sheets investigate possibilities without committing them to the language.

---

# P

## Parameter

A characteristic whose value may vary while remaining consistent with the Grammar.

Parameters define controlled variation rather than arbitrary change.

---

## Pattern

A reusable geometric solution that has demonstrated lasting value within the language.

Patterns are recorded in the Pattern Library.

---

## Pattern Library

The curated collection of reusable Patterns accepted into the Grammar.

The Pattern Library represents the project's growing geometric vocabulary.

---

## Primary Structure

The first generation of geometry established from the Center.

Primary Structure provides the framework from which later Growth occurs.

---

# S

## Seasonal Parameter

A parameter that distinguishes one glyph from another while remaining compatible with the shared Grammar.

Seasonal Parameters express identity without altering the underlying language.

---

## Specification

A technical document defining how a portion of the language is organized or constructed.

Specifications describe architecture rather than philosophy.

---

## Symmetry

A structural relationship between repeated geometric elements.

Glyph Garden recognizes symmetry as a property of construction rather than ornament.

---

# V

## Vocabulary

The collection of Patterns, Modules, Assemblies, and Glyphs discovered through application of the Grammar.

Where the Grammar defines what *can* exist, the Vocabulary records what *does* exist.

---

# Relationship to Other Documents

The Glossary supports every other document in the repository.

When terminology evolves, definitions should be updated here before they are adopted elsewhere.

---

> *Discover forms. Don't invent them.*
