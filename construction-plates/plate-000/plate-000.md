# Plate 000 — Construction Universe

**Project:** Glyph Garden  
**Language:** Grammar of the Seasons  
**Plate:** 000  
**Document Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** Draft

---

## Purpose

Plate 000 establishes the geometric universe within which every Glyph Garden construction exists.

Unlike later Plates, it introduces no Modules, Growth, or Assemblies.

Its sole purpose is to define the space in which those concepts may eventually appear.

Everything constructed within the Grammar begins here.

---

# Concept Introduced

## Construction Universe

The Construction Universe is the shared geometric environment used by every glyph.

It establishes:

- a common origin,
- a common orientation,
- a common scale,
- a common visual language.

No expressive geometry exists yet.

Only possibility.

---

# Why This Plate Exists

Every language assumes a world.

Before discussing stems, branches, or symmetry, the project must first answer a simpler question:

> *Where do these things exist?*

Plate 000 intentionally postpones every interesting decision in order to establish one uninteresting—but essential—foundation.

Future Plates should inherit this universe rather than redefine it.

---

# What This Plate Introduces

Plate 000 establishes:

- the construction boundary,
- the primary center,
- canonical orientation,
- reference scale.

It deliberately avoids introducing:

- branching,
- modules,
- repetition,
- hierarchy,
- symmetry order,
- seasonal characteristics.

Those belong to later Plates.

---

# Design Notes

Plate 000 should feel almost empty.

Its value comes from restraint.

A successful Plate 000 provides just enough structure to support every future construction while making as few assumptions as possible.

When viewed in isolation it may appear incomplete.

That is intentional.

---

# Questions Explored

During development, the following questions informed Plate 000.

- Where should construction begin?
- What belongs in the universal geometry?
- Which decisions should be postponed?
- How little geometry is necessary before meaningful construction becomes possible?

These questions continue to guide future Plates.

---

# Dependencies

None.

Plate 000 is the root of the Construction Plate sequence.

---

# Enables

Plate 000 enables:

- Plate 001 — Primary Structure
- Plate 002 — Growth Stations
- Plate 003 — Branch Vocabulary
- Plate 004 — Rotational Assembly

Every subsequent Plate assumes the Construction Universe established here.

---

# Success Criteria

Plate 000 succeeds if:

- every future Plate can inherit it unchanged,
- no seasonal assumptions are embedded,
- the Construction Universe remains visually neutral,
- later discoveries do not require redefining its foundation.

---

# Future Evolution

Future revisions may clarify the Construction Universe.

They should avoid introducing expressive geometry.

Whenever possible, new discoveries should occur in later Plates rather than expanding Plate 000.
